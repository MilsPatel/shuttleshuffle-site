Shuttle Shuffle

Clear the lot. Fill every seat.

Shuttle Shuffle is a parking puzzle for Android, iPhone and iPad. Tap a car to drive it out of the lot, park it in a free spot, and let passengers of the same colour climb aboard. Plan your moves so you never run out of spots.

Free to play

No accounts and no purchases. The game is paid for by ads: a short ad between some levels, and optional videos you can watch for extra coins, spots or boosters. Your progress stays on your device.
