Shuttle Shuffle

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026. This policy covers the Shuttle Shuffle game for Android, iPhone and iPad.

The short version

Shuttle Shuffle does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not need an internet connection to play.

What stays on your device

The game saves your level, coins, boosters and sound setting in a small file on your device so you can continue where you left off. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Third parties

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity analytics and crash reporting are turned off, and the game contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. If you open this page from the game, your browser and the website host (GitHub Pages) handle that visit under their own policies.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
