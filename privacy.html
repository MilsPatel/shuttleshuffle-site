Honkalot

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026. This policy covers the Honkalot game for Android, iPhone and iPad.

The short version

Honkalot itself does not collect any personal data and has no accounts or in-app purchases. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below. You can play offline; ads only appear when you are online.

What stays on your device

The game saves your level, coins, boosters, sound and music settings in a small file on your device so you can continue where you left off. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows a full-screen ad between some levels, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward. These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier, your approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with the ads, and crash and performance data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

The game asks Google for family-safe ads only.

Your choices

On iPhone and iPad the game asks whether it may track you (“Allow tracking?”). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads still appear but are not personalised with your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the game first asks for your consent, and you can change your choice later under Settings › Privacy choices in the game.

Permissions

Apart from the tracking question above, the game asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Third parties

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity analytics and crash reporting are turned off. The only third-party SDK that receives data is Google AdMob (see above). If you open this page from the game, your browser and the website host (GitHub Pages) handle that visit under their own policies.

Children

The game’s content suits all ages, but the game is not directed at children under 13. It requests family-safe ads only. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and want Google’s ad data about that device removed, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
